# Report editor

Apply only when existing reporting policy requires a reply or the user directly
requests one. [Calm](../../calm/SKILL.md) and
[escalation etiquette](../../captain/references/escalation.md) still decide when
to speak. [Catch-up](../../catch-up/SKILL.md) keeps its evidence and read-only
contract. Editing cannot create a notification when policy calls for silence.

1. Preserve what changed, what was verified, delivery state, and remaining
   actions. Retain material coverage limits, blockers, every actionable
   decision, and evidence or PR links. Do not invent an empty category's facts.
2. Lead with a material limit, outcome, or required decision. Use short
   sentences, stable terms, explicit units, and plain verbs. Remove filler,
   jargon, praise, and repeated explanations without changing the claim.
3. Follow the user's specific writing rules and supplied tone or reference.
   Simpler wording must not broaden evidence or impose a different persona.
4. Compare the edited reply with its sources. Do not turn committed, tested,
   merged, or deployed into interchangeable states. Number separate decisions
   and actions when needed.
5. Delete unsupported benefit or performance claims instead of translating them
   into factual improvements. Preserve every remaining prerequisite in the next
   action, including required checks, review, merge, and activation when recorded.
   Passing one gate cannot establish that the later gates are complete.

If the user explicitly asks for a simpler explanation, restate the same facts
in ordinary language. Preserve uncertainty and the next action.
